Blase Ullrich

Host & Producer

Hey y'all welcome back to the chalked sports podcast!

In October of 2024, I met members of the Deep 3 podcast at a Bulls game; then by December, I was recording my first episode with Tanner on the back porch of my house. I've always had a passion for sports but never the motivation to start until that day. After that, it's been a project that I've had a passion for, posting 66 episodes. The mission is simple: keep sports happy and stay away from stiff or hot take media. Say things you believe and defend them, not to say stuff for the sake of clips. I also aim to make every sport fan be seen; from NFL to WNBA, I'll talk about it all as long as I have access to it. Chalked Sports is about the love of sports, from a fan, unfiltered, honest, and analytical.

🏈 NFL & College Football

Next-morning breakdowns, deep analytical dives into coverage schemes, and honest, unfiltered hot takes.

🏀 NBA, WNBA, CBB & NHL

Smart research, high-leverage matchup angles, and positive energy built for every sports fan.
